      Is it legal to remove a possum in Victoria?

      Yes, a possum can be removed from your roof, but only in a legal way. Possums are protected under the Wildlife Act 1975. A trapped possum must be released within 50 metres of where it was caught, after sunset, on the same day.

      That rule is why proofing matters. A possum released 50 metres away comes straight back to an open roof. Removal and proofing go together for that reason.

      Signs you have a possum in the roof

      POSSUM-ANIMAL
      • Heavy thumps or footsteps across the ceiling at dusk and before dawn
      • Scratching or hissing sounds near the eaves
      • Droppings on paths, the deck or the roof, larger than rat droppings
      • Brown urine stains on the ceiling
      • A strong smell in one room
      • Loose tiles, a bent gutter or a gap at the roofline

      Light, fast scratching at any hour points more to rats. The noise checker above helps you tell them apart. Rats in the roof are a job for rodent control.

      Remove the possum or proof the roof?

      Many roofs need both. The right choice depends on whether the possum is still inside and how many gaps the roof has.

      Removal alone may make sense if:

      • The possum is inside now and the entry point is a single small gap
      • The roof was proofed before and only one spot has failed
      • The possum is in a shed or garage, not the house

      Proofing may make more sense if:

      • The possum has already left, but you hear one on the roof most nights
      • Possums have come back after an earlier removal
      • The roof has several loose tiles, gaps or a damaged gutter

      Possum removal questions

      Will a possum eventually leave?

      No, not for good. A possum may leave the roof at night to feed, but it comes back to the same roof while the entry point stays open. A possum treats a dry roof as its den. The way to stop the visits for good is to wait until the possum is out, or use a one-way door, and then seal the gap.

      How much does possum removal cost?

      Possum removal in Box Hill usually costs $265 to $420. The price depends on how many entry points the roof has, how high and easy the roof is to reach, and whether proofing is part of the job. Your exact quote comes before any work starts.

      What does a possum in your roof sound like?

      A possum in the roof sounds like heavy footsteps or thumps, mostly at dusk and before dawn when the possum leaves and comes back. You may also hear hissing, growling or scratching near the eaves. Rats sound lighter and faster. Use the noise checker on this page if you are not sure.

      Does a possum smell bad?

      Yes, a possum in the roof can smell bad. The smell comes from possum urine and droppings in the insulation, and the smell can stain the ceiling. A dead possum smells much stronger. Removing the possum, cleaning the area and sealing the entry stop the smell from building up again.

      Can a possum live in your roof?

      Yes, a possum can live in your roof. A roof space is dry and warm, so a brushtail possum may use the roof as its den and come back every morning. A possum gets in through loose tiles, gaps under the eaves or a damaged gutter. Proofing those gaps stops the possum living there.
