      Possum in roof removal in Burwood

      For a house roof, a one-way door is fitted over the gap the possum uses. The possum walks out at night to feed and cannot get back in. Once the roof is quiet, the door comes off and the gap is closed.

      Possum proofing in Burwood

      Proofing seals the roofline with metal mesh and flashing, so no possum can find a new way in. Proofing protects the roof after a removal and also protects a roof where possums only run across at night.

      Is possum work in Burwood guaranteed?

      How much does possum removal cost in Burwood?

      A Burwood possum job usually costs $265 to $420, the same range as Box Hill. The final figure depends on the number of gaps in the roof, roof height and access, and whether the roof needs full proofing. You get the exact quote before any work starts.

      Where do I take an injured possum in Burwood?

      Do not pick up an injured possum with bare hands, because a hurt possum can scratch and bite. Keep pets inside and call a wildlife rescue group or a local vet, who can arrange care. A possum that is not hurt but is in your roof is a removal job, so call 0401 042 325.

      How do you discourage possums from a Burwood roof?

      The most reliable way to discourage possums is to close the gaps they use and trim branches that touch the roof. Lights and sprays may move a possum for a few nights but do not close the roof. A possum box in a tree can give the possum another place to sleep.

      Are possums marsupials?

      Yes, possums are marsupials. A female possum carries her young in a pouch, and later on her back. The brushtail possum and the ringtail possum are the two kinds found in Melbourne suburbs such as Burwood. Both are protected under the Wildlife Act 1975.
