      How is pest control done at home?

      Pest control at home starts with an inspection to find the pest, where it lives and how it gets in. The technician then treats those places with the product or method that suits that pest. You get advice on how to keep the pest out. The exact steps depend on the pest, so the technician explains them before starting.

      Is pest control safe for children and pets?

      Yes, pest control from Melbourne East Pest Control is safe for children and pets. We use low-toxic products and tell you when each room is safe to use again. The technician tells you which rooms to keep children and pets out of, and for how long, before the treatment starts. Ask about your own pets when you book, so the technician can plan the treatment around them.

      Why do I suddenly have so many cockroaches?

      A sudden rise in cockroaches can come from warm weather, a new source of food or water, or cockroaches moving in from a neighbouring property. German cockroaches can breed inside a kitchen, so a small number can grow fast. Treatment works best when the nest areas are found first, so an inspection comes before any spray.

      Should you leave huntsman spiders in your house?

      Yes, a huntsman spider can be left in the house, because a huntsman eats insects and rarely bites people. Many people still want huntsman spiders out, and that choice is yours. You can catch one under a container and put it outside. A treatment around doors, windows and eaves helps if spiders keep coming inside.
